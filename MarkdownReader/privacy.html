AI Doc Reader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

  • All document rendering happens locally on your Mac. Your files never leave your device.
  • The app contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, and no user accounts.
  • The app makes no network connections to our servers — we operate none.
  • Files are accessed only when you open them, under macOS App Sandbox protection, in read-only mode.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact lj_dev@ymail.ne.jp.
